My time at Krai was incredibly well spent and it can be described from the following aspects:

# Why do we need benchmarking?
Benchmarking is extremetly important for the research community and the industry. To researcher, it is a mean of proving their work, to add solid incremental values to the community. To industry, more performant system improve efficiency and provides financial gains. At Krai, we use MLPerf as a platform to help chip vendors/ AI accelerators R&D company to showcase their ML systems, especially from an end-to-end perspective, achieveing software-hardware co-design. Most benchmarking has been forcusing on either software optimisation or hardware optimisation, but it is important to look at the system as a whole to maximize gains. There is a saying in the company on how we describe our work, if MLPerf is the olympics of ML systems, we are the olympic coach. There are mainly two matric to measure an ML system, accuracy and speed (which we commonly referred to as performance), and we measured these things on how well they run different machine learning models (inference time) and how quickly they train models to attain certain accuracy (training time)

# How do we do that?
I and the team has been actively developing and maintaining an open souce product called axs, which is our unique proof-of-concept product that enable plug-and-play models, hardware, software deck of ML systems - the pinacle of software-hardware co-design. 

# Life in a start-up
Working with more than 1 person on a software product means that we have to uphold coding standards, maintain clean documentation, testing and in general advocate for clean code practices. I have set up and heavily upgraded the CI platform for axs.

# Project management
I was managing 4 projects at the same time at some point. To stay on top of things, I have utilised JIRA practices and project management tools to mentor junior engineers and new starters. I also tailored starter projects and on-boarding materials that suits their specific background to ensure they have a great time at Krai. 


I am forever greatful for the opportunities and supports Krai has given me. They are a bunch of really talented people, follow their latest work here!
